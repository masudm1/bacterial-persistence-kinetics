# Original experimental data sources

The original third-party files are not redistributed in this repository.
Download them from the official locations below. The processed tables used to
regenerate the manuscript figures are included in `figure_code/figure_data/`.

## Umetani et al.

**Study:** *Observation of persister cell histories reveals diverse modes of
survival in antibiotic persistence*

**Dataset:** Dryad, <https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.s1rn8pkb1>

Download and extract `DataForDryad_Uploaded.zip`. The analysis uses population
tables including `Fig8S2.xlsx`, `Fig1S3.xlsx`, and `Fig3.xlsx`, together with
tracking files under `Tracking_x40/` for the single-cell lag analysis.
